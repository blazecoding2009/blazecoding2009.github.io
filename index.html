Warrig X2 · Electrathon Race Car

2025 — Present

Vehicle
Single-seat electric

Single-seat electric race car competing in the Electrathon Canada series. I lead mechanical and electrical — the rear drivetrain redesign was the focal point of the 2025 season.

  • Replaced a failing brushed single-motor system with three Kraken X60 brushless motors sharing a West Coast Products gearbox at an 11.46:1 total reduction (12:60 internal stage, 24:55 chain stage).
  • Designed the motor-mount bracket through a full iteration cycle — started with a 3D-printed PLA prototype that exposed a structural failure mode in the bolt pattern, then redesigned and had the final version machined from aluminum.
  • Owned CAN bus layout, breaker sizing (120A Bussmann HI-AMP), and per-motor current limiting via Talon FX telemetry.
  • Authored the full Engineering Design Report submitted to competition.
