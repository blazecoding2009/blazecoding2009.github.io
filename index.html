About Me

I'm Arnnav Kudale, a Grade 12 student based in Oakville, Ontario, Canada, working at the intersection of mechanical and electrical engineering. I'm President of the White Oaks Electrathon team, where I lead mechanical and electrical on our competition electric race car, and a build and design member of FRC 1360 Orbit Robotics, where I led electrical for the 2026 robot and was the first student certified on the Tormach 1100MX CNC mill. I spent a summer at DRDO developing autonomous drone software with ROS2, and I founded TensorForest, a drone-based wildfire risk mapping project. Outside of build season I organize hackathons, mentor FLL teams, and serve as Training Chief with RCSCC 178 Oakville. I'm currently open to mechanical or electrical engineering internships.
